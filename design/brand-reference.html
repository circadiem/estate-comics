Provenance

Heritage meets precision. The rendered specimen of the Estate Comics design system.

Archival design, museum catalogues, and the quiet confidence of institutions that have done one thing well for a very long time. The audience is an executor, an attorney, or an adult child holding a longbox they did not ask for. Nothing here should suggest a hobby shop.

The wordmark's gold hairline is part of the logo, not decoration around it. It recurs as the section rule, the table header rule, the PDF header, and the email divider. That repetition is what makes the brand read as one thing.

Cormorant gets fragile below 20px — never use it under 18px except in the wordmark. Give serif text a little more line-height than the sans. Body measure 62–72 characters. No third family, and no monospace face for data labels.

Rules

The short list that keeps this from drifting into a template.

✓
One border radius — 2px — on everything. Not zero, not rounded.
✓
Elevation is a 1px bisque border and an ivory fill on the vellum ground.
✓
Left-aligned throughout. Only the wordmark and the PDF header are centered.
✓
One animated moment: books resolving into the results feed as photos process.
✕
No drop shadows, no gradients, no second accent color.
✕
No tracked-out all-caps eyebrow labels. The italic Cormorant subhead does that job.
✕
No arrow glyphs appended to link or button text.
✕
Nothing comic-book-y: no primary colors, halftone, or bold slab display.
✕
No reference to The Corner Box, anywhere, in any form.
